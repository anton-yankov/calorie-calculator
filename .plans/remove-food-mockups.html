Removing a food from an AI estimate

When the AI splits a sandwich into bread, ham, cheese and butter, the only fix today is setting the cheese to 0 g or asking the AI to try again. Here are four quick ways to drop an item and undo it. All four mockups work, so tap them.

Screen: the estimate card on the homepage Code: EstimateCard.tsx, AnalysisProvider.tsx Data: no database change

01What you see today

Each row has a name, a confidence tag, and a grams field. You can tap the name to see the AI's assumptions. There's no way to remove a row. Typing 0 g sort of works, but the cheese still shows up in the meal and is saved with it.

    02Option A: × on every row, undo in place

    Every row gets a small × to the right of the grams field. Tap it and the row shrinks to a dashed line, "Cheese removed · Undo", in the same spot. The undo line stays until you log the meal, so there's no timer to race. It's one tap to remove, one tap to undo, and it works the same with a mouse.

      03Option B: Swipe left to delete

      This is how iOS Mail does it. Drag a row left to reveal a red Delete. Let go past halfway (or tap Delete) and the row is gone. A toast slides up from the bottom of the card with Undo for 5 seconds. The rows look exactly like they do today.

        04Option C: Remove inside the row's details

        Tapping a food's name already opens its details (the AI's assumptions, carbs and fat). This adds a "Remove from meal" button there. The removed food stays in the list, struck through, and its grams field turns into "Add back". Nothing new appears on the card until you open a row.

          05Option D: A checkmark on every food

          Every food starts with a green check. Tap the check and the food is left out of the meal: it's dimmed, struck through, and the header says "3 of 4 foods". Tap it again to bring it back. Removing and undoing are the same button.

            07Same in every option

            • Totals update at once. The calories, macros, weight and "After this meal" line all drop by the food's amount as soon as it's removed, with the green ▼ you already see after corrections.
            • Undo restores everything exactly. The food comes back in its old spot with the grams you had typed.
            • Removing every food greys out Log and the card says "Nothing left to log". Start over still works.
            • Asking the AI for a correction after removing something sends the meal without that food, so the new estimate starts from what you kept. Any pending undo is cleared at that point.
            • Barcode-scanned items added to the meal can be removed the same way.
            • Nothing is saved until you tap Log. Removed foods never reach the database, so no SQL is needed.

            One open question

            Meals you've already logged open in the Edit meal sheet, which has grams fields but no remove either. Whichever option you pick could be added there too, using the same look. Do you want that in the same change, or only on the estimate card for now?
