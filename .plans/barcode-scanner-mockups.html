Barcode scanner · design options

Three calmer scanners

The scanner is the last screen still wearing the old look. Here are three directions for it, from one line to none. Each one is shown scanning and at the moment it finds a code. Pick one and I'll build it.

What's not working

  • Two lines that cross. They run out of phase, so the frame never settles. Two lines were meant to say "upright or sideways", but the reader turns the frame itself (barcode-reader.ts), so you don't need to line anything up.
  • Leftover old colours. The scrims use the old brown rgba(27,26,22,…) and the glow is the old terracotta #E08A5C, not the new peach.
  • The camera gets squeezed. The header bar and the always-open number form take about 250px of an 812px screen.
  • The flashlight button is the text "○ Light" rather than an icon.
