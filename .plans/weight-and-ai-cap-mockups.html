02How the trend line works

Scale weight jumps around by a kilo or more from day to day (water, salt, digestion). The trend line is an exponential moving average: each weigh-in moves the trend only part of the way toward itself, so single spikes barely move it while a real change pulls it along within a week or two.

Weigh-inPrevious trendNew trend (10% of the way)
84.6 kg84.0 kg84.0 + 0.10 × (84.6 − 84.0) = 84.06 kg
83.2 kg84.06 kg84.06 + 0.10 × (83.2 − 84.06) = 83.97 kg
  • Days without a weigh-in are skipped — the trend simply waits for the next one.
  • "Trend change" compares the trend at the start and end of the range, not two raw weigh-ins, so one heavy morning can't flip the number.
  • Weight entries never change your plan. When you change your plan in Settings, its weight field starts at your latest weigh-in.

05When the analyses run out

Only the AI stops. Photo analysis, typed descriptions and corrections are paused until midnight, and so is the nutrition-label scan in the barcode panel. Barcodes, water ("water 500"), logging and quick entry keep working, and the message says so.
