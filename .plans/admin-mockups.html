Admin: Users & User Detail

The admin area only your account can open: a list of everyone with their AI use and an editable daily cap, and a read-only page per user with their plan, log, stats and products.

Access: admin role only — anyone else gets "page not found"Writes: only the AI cap

01Getting there

An "Admin" section at the bottom of Settings, visible only to you, links to /admin. The nav stays as it is — it's already full on phones, and you'll visit the admin pages far less often than the tracker.

02Users list

One row per account. "Last active" is the latest logged meal. The cap is edited in place; "Save" writes it at once. Your own row shows "∞" instead of a cap field.

03User detail (read-only)
