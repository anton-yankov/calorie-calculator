Showing how much the meal weighs

After a photo or description, the estimate shows calories and macros for the whole meal but never its total weight. Here are four ways to add it, plus how it would carry over to meals you've already logged.

Screen: the estimate card on the homepage Also affects: Eaten today, the Log Data: already stored, no database change

01What you see today

The AI already estimates grams for every item, and they're the boxes on the right of each row. You can type a real weight there and that row recalculates on the spot. But the meal's total weight isn't shown anywhere, and once you log the meal its weight drops out of sight.

04Option C: Total weight you can edit

This one fixes accuracy as well as showing the weight. Under the totals sits a "Total weight" box filled in with the AI's guess. If you weighed the plate, type the real number and every item scales to match, so calories update right away. It doesn't use an AI analysis.

The catch with C: scaling keeps the AI's split between items. If it saw 180 g of rice and 150 g of chicken, a heavier plate means more of both in that ratio. When the split itself is wrong ("it's mostly rice"), typing a weight in a single row or writing a correction to the AI still works better. Option C fixes the common case, where the AI has the right items but the wrong amount.

05Option D: Weight breakdown bar

A strip above the totals shows the total weight in large type, with a bar showing how it splits across the items. It shows the most at a glance and helps you spot a wrong split, such as the AI giving you far too much rice.

07Odd cases (same in every option)

CaseWhat the total shows
Meal with a drinkDrinks are measured in ml, so they stay separate rather than being added to the grams: 620 kcal · 430 g + 300 ml. In Option C, the total-weight box scales only the food. Each drink keeps its own ml box.
Drinks onlyJust the volume (300 ml), with no gram figure.
Barcode itemsCounted like any other item. Their grams come from the portion you chose when scanning.
Manual entriesThese have no weight (you type calories directly), so they're left out of the total. If every item is manual, the weight is hidden instead of showing 0 g.
Older logged mealsThey work too. The grams were always saved with the meal, just never added up.
