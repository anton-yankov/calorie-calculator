The Settings Page

One place for the plan, the body details behind it, water tracking and the account. Reached from a gear in the top bar, since the four tabs already fill the row on a phone.

03The rules behind it

RuleWhy
Editing details never moves targetsYour decision: targets change only when you change them. Saving a new weight shows a note offering a plan change instead of silently rewriting today's numbers.
A plan change starts todayEarlier days keep the plan they were logged under, so history stays honest.
Water off ≠ drinks ignoredA latte still adds calories and protein; only the water bar and water stats disappear.
Changing the password asks for the current oneSomeone holding your unlocked phone can't lock you out.
Log out stays at the bottomIt moves off the top bar (where it lives now), so the tab row gets its space back.
"Change plan" reuses the setup flow's goal step and plan cards rather than a second implementation, so any change to the plan picker shows up in both places.
