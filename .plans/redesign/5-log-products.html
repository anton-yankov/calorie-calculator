Log & Products

The two list pages. Both keep everything they do today; what changes is that every action becomes a real button, and editing happens in a sheet instead of tiny inline fields.

Same data as mocks 2 and 4 Your picks: 2 questions in section 05
Decided: as shown, plus "Log it now" on products and search on Products (shown once there are more than ~6). No g/ml switch: the unit follows the product (ml for drinks, g otherwise).

01What changes

  • No big header, no manual-entry panel. Manual add moved to the homepage (mock 2), so the Log is only your days.
  • Each day is a card with its status chip ("On track", "Over by 140", "910 left") and two slim bars, followed by that day's meals in the same rows as the homepage.
  • Tap a meal to open it: photo, every food with its weight, macros, and three real buttons: Edit · Log again · Delete.
  • Edit opens a sheet with big weight fields, day and time, and the new total before you save. Today it's 56px inputs inside the row.
  • ⋯ on each row gives the same actions without opening the meal.
  • Products get a search field, a row per product with its default amount, and the same ⋯ / sheet pattern.
