Users (admin)

Only you see these pages. They look better and are easier to scan; they do exactly what they do today: see every account read-only, and change the daily AI cap.

Opens from: Settings → Users and AI caps No new actions, no new data
Decided: as shown · cap quick picks Pause (0) · 10 · 20 · 50 · 100 + custom up to 1000.

01What changes

  • Three totals on top: accounts, active today, AI analyses used today.
  • One card per account with an activity dot (green = ate today, grey = quiet, empty = not set up), the plan, and AI use as a bar against the cap. It turns amber at 3 or fewer left, as the user's own counter does.
  • The cap is a button ("Cap 20 a day") that opens a sheet with quick picks, instead of a tiny number box and a small Save.
  • User detail: a "Read-only" chip instead of the amber banner, three key numbers at the top, and Overview · Log · Stats · Products as the same switch Stats uses.
  • Everything shown comes from data the page already loads.
