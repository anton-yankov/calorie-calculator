Stats

Stats rebuilt around one question: how am I doing? It answers that at the top, puts the charts where you can see them, and gives weight its own space.

Example: losing, 2,150 kcal · 125 g protein, last 30 days Status: decided
Decided: S1, with Nutrition · Weight · Water as separate sections · insight card with the five fixed checks (no AI) · keep the "at your pace so far" goal estimate · smooth trend curve · large dates in the calendar, one calendar per month on 90d and All.

01What changes

  • Three sections instead of one long scroll: Nutrition · Weight · Water, switched at the top. Water only appears when tracking is on.
  • The answer comes first: "18 of 26 days on track", with how that compares to the 30 days before.
  • New: a calendar of your days, coloured by how each one went. You see streaks and bad weekends at a glance.
  • Charts come second, not seventh. Today they sit under eight tiles and the weight form.
  • Weight shows the journey: start → now → goal, where your current pace gets you, and whether that matches your plan.
  • The weight form becomes a button that opens the same sheet as the homepage. Weigh-ins get ⋯ instead of Edit/Delete text links.

04How the insight card works

No AI. It's a fixed set of checks, written in code, that run on the same numbers the charts use. Each check has a sentence template; the page shows the most important one that passes, or no card at all.

CheckShows whenExample sentence
Weekday pattern4+ days over the limit, and 60%+ of them fall on the same two weekdays"7 of your 8 days over the limit were a Friday or Saturday."
Protein streakProtein target hit 5+ finished days in a row, ending yesterday"6 days in a row at your protein target. Your longest run yet."
Protein gapAverage protein is 15+ g under target over 7+ logged days"You're usually about 20 g short on protein. One extra yogurt or shake would close it."
Getting betterDays on track up by 3+ against the period before"18 days on track, up from 15 the 30 days before."
Logging gaps3+ days not logged in the last 7"3 of the last 7 days weren't logged, so these numbers may look better than they are."
  • One card at a time, chosen in the order above (most specific first). Nothing passes → no card.
  • Instant, free and private: worked out in the browser from your own data. It doesn't use any of your 20 AI analyses and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Never wrong about numbers: every number in the sentence is filled in from the same calculation as the charts.
  • Follows the range: switching 7d → 30d re-runs the checks on that range.
Why not AI? It would cost an analysis each time Stats opens, take seconds instead of instantly, and could word numbers wrongly. Fixed checks can only say things that are true. AI could be added later as an optional "explain my month" button if you ever want it.
