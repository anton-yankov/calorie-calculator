Settings

Your account page, rebuilt as a short list. The plan stays on top; everything else is one row that opens its own screen or sheet, instead of five long forms stacked on one page.

Opens from: the profile button at the top right Your picks: 2 questions in section 05
Decided: as shown · reminder options daily / 3 / 7 (default) / 14 days / off, saved on the account (new column, SQL at build time) · water goal saves by itself · icons in the real build come from an icon library (none is installed, so lucide-react gets added).

01What changes

  • Plan card on top with your targets, goal date and protein level, and Change plan as the main button.
  • Grouped rows (You · Tracking · Account · Admin). Each shows its current value, so you can read your settings without opening anything.
  • New: Weigh-in reminder, the setting the homepage card links to (mock 2).
  • Water tracking is a real toggle right in the list; the goal field appears under it when it's on.
  • Log out is its own red row, not a big outlined button at the end of a long form.
  • Everything that works today stays: the plan history, "your plan was built at 85.3 kg" hint, weight pre-filled from the latest weigh-in, password check, admin link.
