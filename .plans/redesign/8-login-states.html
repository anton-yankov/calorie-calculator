02App icon: pick one

The icon on your home screen, the browser tab and the login page. Today's icon is brown and orange, which won't match the new plum and peach. All three keep the same idea: a gauge on a plate.

My pick is I2: it matches the app exactly and reads well on both light and dark wallpapers. I3 is the one that stands out most on a home screen. The installed app's theme colour (status bar, splash screen) changes to the plum background either way.
