01What stays the same in every option

Each of these fixes something from the review, whichever style you pick.

  • Pages stay at the top, but bigger and easier to tap than today: 44px targets and 13–14px labels instead of 11px. Settings sits behind the round icon at the top right. Section 02 has three ways to do it.
  • No big page header. The page starts with your numbers, not with a 2.9rem title and a thick rule.
  • Calories left as the headline number. Calorie and protein bars keep today's goal colours (blue when losing, green when met, amber when close, red when over).
  • Four ways to add food, all as real buttons: Photo, Describe, Barcode, Manual. Manual add moves here from the Log page.
  • AI analyses left sit next to the AI buttons. The strip under the nav goes away.
  • The weigh-in reminder can save the weight on the spot. (Its details come in mock 2.)
  • Filled cards with larger corners instead of thin borders everywhere. Labels are readable sentence case, not 10px capitals.
The homepage layout here is a first draft so the styles have something real to show. Order, the analyzing seconds counter and the reminder get their own mock next. Judge the look here.

03Buttons

One set of rules so nothing that does something ever looks like plain text again. Shown in style A; the shapes stay the same in B and C, only the colours change.

Start this planPrimary: the one main action on a screen. Filled accent. At most one per screen. Change planSecondary: important, but not the main thing. Tinted fill. EditOutline: everyday actions like Edit, Cancel and Back. Always has a visible border. DeleteDestructive: red tint, and always undoable through the toast. More (⋯): opens a sheet when a row has 3+ actions, instead of a row of text links.
  • Every tap target is at least 44px tall (46px for main buttons).
  • Plain text links only for navigation inside a sentence, never for actions.
  • Labels say what happens: "Save weight", not "Submit"; the toast repeats it ("Weight saved").
