Setup

The three setup steps in style C, with the two things your friend ran into fixed: protein you can choose, and a Custom plan that looks like a real option instead of a line of orange text.

Example person: born 1994, 180 cm, 84.6 kg, moderately active, losing to 78 kg Your pick: P1, P2 or P3 in section 03
Decided: P1 (segmented bar) · protein from the goal weight when losing, current weight otherwise · levels Light 1.2 / Moderate 1.6 (recommended) / High 2.0 g per kg · Custom plan under the maintenance number.

01What changes

  • Protein is a choice. Three levels instead of a fixed 2.0 g per kg. Section 03 has two ways to show it.
  • Custom plan is a card right under your maintenance number, before the protein and pace choices, so you see it straight away. Same size as the plan cards, with a pencil icon and a chevron.
  • "How we got this" is a button on the maintenance card, not a grey link.
  • Back sits at the top left as a button on every step, next to the step count. Log out (in case it's the wrong account) is an outline button at the top right.
  • The main button stays at the bottom of the screen (Continue / Start this plan), so you never have to scroll to find it.
  • Settings → Change plan uses the same plan step, so it gets the protein choice too.

03Step 3 · protein: pick P1, P2 or P3

Protein comes first on this step, because the plan cards below it show the result. All three options offer the same three levels. The Custom plan card now sits right under your maintenance number in each of them.

When losing weight, the grams are worked out from your goal weight (78 kg), not your current weight. That's why High shows 156 g here instead of today's 169 g. Maintain and gain use your current weight. Anything else can be typed in through Custom plan.

06What I need from you

  1. Protein: P1 (segmented bar), P2 (slim slider) or P3 (stepper)?My pick is P1: every level and its grams are visible at once, and it's one tap. That's the easiest for someone setting up for the first time.
  2. Base protein on the goal weight when losing?Recommended: it keeps the target realistic for people with more to lose. The alternative is always using the current weight, as today.
  3. Are the levels and wording right?Light 1.2 · Moderate 1.6 (recommended) · High 2.0 g per kg.
