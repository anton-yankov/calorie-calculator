  1. Title and tabsThe date and "Today", the profile button (opens Settings), then Home · Log · Stats · Products underlined. They stick to the top while you scroll. The AI strip under the nav is gone.
  2. Your numbersCalories left and protein to go are the two big numbers. The bars keep today's goal colours and the tick at your target. The words change with the status: "120 over", "On track", "Target reached".
  3. Add foodPhoto is the big peach button because it's the most used. Describe, Barcode and Manual sit under it at equal size, so Manual is as easy to find as anything else. The AI count lives on the Photo button, the only place it matters.
  4. Weigh-in reminderOnly when it's due. You type the weight and save right here; section 05 has the details.
  5. Eaten todayToday's meals, newest last. Each meal's ⋯ opens Edit amounts / Log again / Delete. The whole history stays on the Log page.
Gone from today's homepage: the big "What's on your plate?" header, the separate description field and Analyze button that were always visible (they're now behind Describe), and "Clear everything" (now Start over, shown only while a meal is in progress).

The bars show calories at 48% because the track runs to 120% of the target, as it does today. The tick marks the target.
