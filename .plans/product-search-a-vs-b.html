Header icon or fourth tile?

The same task done both ways: logging your milk biscuits from the homepage. Each row below is one moment in that task. Option A is always on the left and Option B on the right.

A: magnifying glass next to the profile button, expands into a field B: a fourth "Search" tile next to Barcode, opens a sheet

Before you search. A adds one icon to the top bar. B changes the row of tiles from three to four.

04In the middle of a meal

Say you've described "coffee with milk" and want to add biscuits to the same meal. On phones the tiles hide while a meal is being put together (the meal card takes their place), so B's Search tile hides too. A's icon is in the header, which never hides, so the biscuits go into the meal and the button reads "Add to this meal".

AIcon still thereSearch adds the biscuits to the meal you're building.
BTile hidden with the othersYou'd need to log the coffee first, then search. (On desktop the tiles stay, so B works there.)
This gap in B can be closed: the meal card could get a small "Add a saved product" link. That's more to build, and it puts search in two places.

05One-handed reach

If you hold the phone in your right hand and use your thumb, the bottom half of the screen is easy to reach and the top corners are a stretch. The shading shows roughly where your thumb lands comfortably (green), with effort (yellow) and with a shuffle of the phone (red). The marked button is the one you'd tap to start a search.

ATop right: a stretchThe same spot as the profile button. Fine with two hands.
BMiddle: easyRight next to Barcode, where your thumb already goes.

06On desktop

On a wide screen, Add food is the left column and stays put as you scroll. In A, the icon goes at the right end of the "Today" title row. B's tile is in the left column with the others. On desktop the tiles stay visible during a meal, so B keeps working there.

AHeader icon, desktopA wide search field opens in the title row, with results dropping down under it.
BFourth tile, desktopSearch sits in the left column with the other tiles. Tapping it opens the same list as a centred dialog, the way Manual does on desktop.

If it's a tie, B. You'll mostly search from the homepage with one hand, which is where B is strongest, and it reuses what's already there. Choose A if you often add packaged things to a meal you're building (coffee + biscuits as one entry), or if you want the homepage as clean as it is now.
