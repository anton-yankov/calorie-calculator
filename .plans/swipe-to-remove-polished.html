Swipe a food away, then undo it

This is the swipe direction from the earlier mockups, polished. The Delete button never touches the grams field, every movement eases in and out, and the removed row folds away smoothly. Undo uses the same toast the app already shows when you delete a weigh-in.

Screen: the estimate card on the homepage Builds on: option B in remove-food-mockups.html Data: no database change

01Try it

Drag any food left with your finger or mouse. A short drag stops at Delete. A long drag, or a quick flick past halfway, removes the food straight away. Tap Undo in the toast to bring it back.

    03The spacing

    This fixes what you spotted. Before, the red area sat behind the row at full width, so it butted right up against the grams field. Now the pill is the gap: its width is always the distance the row has moved, minus 10 px. The pill also matches the grams field's height and corners, so when it's open the two read as a pair.

    04The motion

    While your finger is down, the row follows it exactly, with no easing, so it never feels laggy. Easing only takes over once you let go.

    05Details that make it feel right

    • Scrolling is never hijacked. The swipe only starts once your finger clearly moves sideways. An up/down drag scrolls the page as usual.
    • One row open at a time. Opening another row, tapping anywhere else, or scrolling closes the open one. Tapping the open row also closes it, rather than focusing its grams field.
    • The hint plays once. On your first couple of estimates, one row nudges left to show the red pill, then settles back. After that the only reminder is "swipe to remove" in the card's subtitle line.
    • Undo restores everything. The food returns in the same position with the grams you'd typed. Remove two foods quickly and each gets its own toast and its own Undo.
    • Locked while busy. Rows don't swipe while the AI is re-estimating or the meal is being logged.
    • Last food removed: Log greys out and the card reads "Nothing left to log". Undo brings it back.
    • Keyboard and screen readers. Each row also has a "Remove Cheddar cheese" button that's invisible until focused, so it works without swiping.
    • No new library. It's built with plain pointer events in the existing food row. The toast is the app's existing sonner toast.
