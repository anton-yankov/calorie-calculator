The experience

Camera to calories in four steps.

The scan happens on the phone. Nutrition lookup and normalization happen safely on the server.

Open the scanner

Tap “Scan barcode.” The installed web app asks once for camera permission, then selects the rear-facing camera.

Decode on-device

ZXing reads common grocery formats—EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, and UPC-E—from the live video. The camera stops as soon as a code is found.

Find the product

The barcode is sent to the app’s own Next.js route, which queries Open Food Facts for the name, brand, image, and nutrition per 100 g.

Confirm quantity and log

You enter how many grams you ate. The app calculates the exact macros, drops them into the current meal result, and keeps the existing gram editor and log flow.

What the phone needs

Two non-negotiables.

01

HTTPS

Camera APIs require a secure origin. A normal production deployment is fine. A plain http://192.168… development URL opened from your phone may not be.

02

Camera permission

iPhone or Android will show the standard permission prompt. If permission is denied, the app should offer manual barcode entry and explain how to re-enable access.

Where the data comes from

The barcode is an ID—not a nutrition label.

Scanning produces a number. Open Food Facts turns that number into a product record. Your app then calculates the eaten portion from nutrition per 100 g.

EAN / UPC → product record → per-100-g values → your portion

Keep a human in the loop.Open Food Facts is crowdsourced. The product may be missing or its serving size may be unreliable, so users should always confirm the name and grams before logging.

Recommended build

Small surface area. Native-feeling result.

  • Lazy-load @zxing/browser only when scanning starts
  • Prefer the rear camera with facingMode: environment
  • Stop all camera tracks on success, close, and page exit
  • Prevent duplicate detections while product lookup runs
  • Proxy Open Food Facts through a Next.js route
  • Ask for grams rather than trusting serving text
  • Map the product directly into the existing MealAnalysis shape
  • Skip the OpenAI call for scanned packaged foods
  • Offer manual code and nutrition entry as fallbacks
  • Test installed and browser modes on both iPhone and Android
