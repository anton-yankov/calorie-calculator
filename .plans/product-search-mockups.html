Finding a saved product from the homepage

You scanned the milk biscuits box once, so the product is saved, but the biscuits themselves have no barcode. Logging them again means going to Products, finding the box and choosing "Log it now". Here are five places a search could go on the homepage. Two of them are an icon that expands.

Screen: the homepage (phone shown; desktop in section 08) Searches: your saved Products, by name or barcode Dashed green outline: what's new

01The homepage today

From top to bottom: the date and title, the tabs, your calories and protein, the big Snap button, then Describe, Barcode and Manual, then water and the day's meals. Your saved products only show up once you scan the same barcode again.

Current homepageNo way to reach a saved product without its barcode.

02Option A: Search icon in the header

A magnifying glass sits next to the profile button. Tap it and it stretches across the title row into a search field. Results drop down in a panel over the page, which dims. Nothing else on the homepage moves.

03Option B: A fourth tile next to Barcode

The row under Snap gets a fourth tile, "Search", so the ways to add food sit together. Tapping it opens a sheet from the bottom. Before you type, the sheet shows your most recently logged products, so the biscuits are often just one tap away.

05Option D: Quick-add row with a search icon that expands

Built for your exact case. Under the tiles is a row of your most-logged products as small chips: tap "Milk biscuits" and the amount sheet opens with last time's amount already filled in. The magnifying glass at the start of the row expands into a search field for anything that isn't a chip, with results right there on the page.

06Option E: Inside the barcode scanner

The homepage doesn't change at all. When you tap Barcode, the scanner gets a second button at the bottom, "Search my products", next to "Type the number instead". The idea: you think "the barcode thing" when you reach for the biscuits, so search is waiting there.

07After you pick a product (same in every option)

Each result row does one of two things. Tap the row to open the amount sheet you already get after a scan, with last time's amount filled in. Tap the + to log last time's amount straight away, with an Undo in the toast. For biscuits you eat the same way every time, the + saves a step.

08On desktop

On a wide screen, Add food is the left column and stays put as you scroll. B, C and D sit in that column naturally. For A, the icon goes at the right end of the "Today" title row, since desktop has no profile button there. E behaves the same as on a phone.

"Recent" order needs one new column recording when each product was last logged. It's a one-line alter table, which I'll give you inline. Without it, every option falls back to A–Z.

My pick: D. You log the same few things over and over, and D puts them one tap from the homepage with the amount you usually have. That beats any search box. Its magnifying glass is the icon that expands that you had in mind, for everything else. If you'd rather keep the homepage exactly as it is, A is the cleanest alternative, and it's also the only one that stays visible while you're building a meal. A and D also combine well.
