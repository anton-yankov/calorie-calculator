Goal Colours, Quick Entry & Stats

How the bars read differently when you're losing, maintaining or gaining; where typing in a food by hand lives on the Log; and the reworked Stats page. The bars below are live: pick a goal, drag the intake, flip between today and a finished day.

Example targets: 2,310 kcal · 170 g protein (your numbers would come from your plan) Maintain band: ±10%

01What "on track" means per goal

The same bar has to answer a different question depending on the goal. The key extra idea: today isn't over yet, so being under a target today is just progress, while being under it on a finished day is a miss.

GoalCaloriesProtein
LoseA ceiling. Under it is good; within 10% of it today is "close to your limit"; over it is over.Always a floor. Reaching it is the win; under it is progress today and "short" on a finished day.
MaintainA band of ±10% around the target. Inside is on track; above is over; below is progress today and "short" on a finished day.
GainA floor. Reaching it is the win; there's no penalty for going above.
The bar's track now runs to 120% of the target, with a tick at the target itself — so "over" is visible as a bar that passes the tick, instead of a bar that's simply full. For maintain, the ±10% band is shaded on the track.

02Two colour styles — try them live

Both styles use the same rules from section 01; they only differ in how the bar looks while you're still working toward a goal.

A · Traffic light — one palette for every goal

B · Goal-tinted — progress takes the goal's colour

Every goal × state at a glance

My recommendation: B · Goal-tinted. It's what you asked for originally — the bars look different depending on the goal — while the outcome colours (green, amber, red) mean the same thing everywhere, so nothing has to be relearned when you switch goals.
