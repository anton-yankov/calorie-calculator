Product & engineering brief

Meal image behavior

How image expansion should work, why barcode images disappear from the Log, and whether Product edits affect meals already recorded.

Status: Diagnosis complete Scope: Analyze · Log · Products Priority: Mobile-first Code changes: None
Summary Image viewer Barcode gap Product edits Recommendation

01Executive summary

The current behavior is internally consistent, but image data is split across two models that never meet when a meal is saved.

Root cause Product images are not copied into meals
Historical behavior Logged meals are independent snapshots
Recommended principle: preserve historical meals exactly as logged, while choosing and snapshotting a meal-cover image at log time.

02Tap-to-expand images

The Analyze photo already opens full-screen. Log thumbnails, scanned-product images, and Product-library images do not.

SurfaceTodayProposed behavior
Analyze photoAlready expandsMove onto the shared viewer without changing the interaction.
Log thumbnailNot expandableTap opens the viewer without toggling the meal card.
Barcode resultNot expandableTap previews the package image before adding it.
Product libraryNot expandableTap previews; a separate button continues to change the image.

Mobile interaction requirements

  1. Open from the image itselfThe whole visible image is the tap target—no small magnifier control.
  2. Use the full viewportDark backdrop, contained image, phone safe-area padding, and no accidental cropping.
  3. Make closing effortlessA visible 44–48px close button, backdrop tap, and Escape on desktop.
  4. Keep actions unambiguousPreviewing and replacing an image remain separate actions.
Quality constraint: Log images are currently saved at roughly 160px and manually uploaded product images at 320px. They can be expanded, but they will look soft. Good full-screen viewing requires a larger stored preview.

03Why barcode images disappear

The image is saved correctly on the product. It is lost from the meal workflow when the product is converted into a food item.

If a meal contains a photographed plate plus a scanned product, the plate photo becomes the Log thumbnail. If the meal is barcode-only, the Log receives no thumbnail and displays the plate placeholder.

Preferred fix

  1. Carry structured source metadataKeep the barcode and product image with the scanned food while it is in the active analysis.
  2. Choose the meal cover at log timeMeal photo first; otherwise the first scanned product image; otherwise no image.
  3. Snapshot the chosen imageHistorical meals should not depend on a product record or an external Open Food Facts URL that can later change.

04Do Product edits change old meals?

No. A logged meal is a snapshot, not a live reference to the Product library.

ActionEffect on previous mealsEffect going forward
Edit product nutritionNoneThe next scan uses the new values.
Edit product nameNoneThe next scan uses the new name.
Edit or remove product imageNone todayThe next scan shows the updated image.
Delete productNoneA later scan falls back to catalog lookup or manual entry.
Log an old meal againCreates a copy of the old snapshotIt does not refresh from Products.
This is the right default for a food diary. Past calories and macros should continue to represent what was recorded at that time, even when the reusable product definition is corrected later.

05Recommended implementation direction

Treat viewing, list thumbnails, and durable image storage as related but separate concerns.

  1. Build one shared image viewerUse it across Analyze, Log, barcode confirmation, Product cards, and product editing.
  2. Add barcode image fallback for meal coversThis resolves the visible bug for barcode-only meals without changing historical nutrition semantics.
  3. Store a larger durable previewPrefer Supabase Storage plus a URL/reference; retain a tiny thumbnail for fast Log rendering.
  4. Keep logged meals immutable by defaultFuture product edits apply only to future scans. Any bulk historical correction should be a separate, explicit tool.

Technical evidence

Meal thumbnail creation

AnalysisProvider.handleLog() derives the thumbnail only from the resized main meal photo. A barcode-only meal therefore logs thumbnail: null.

Product-to-food conversion

barcodeProductToFood() does not copy imageUrl or a structured barcode onto FoodItem. The barcode survives only as prose inside assumptions.

Historical meal storage

The meal table stores its own analysis JSON and thumbnail. Product edits update a separate barcode_products row, and re-logging duplicates the existing meal row.
